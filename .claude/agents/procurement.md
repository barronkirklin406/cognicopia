2. Instantly render and email a Net-30 PDF Invoice featuring:
   - Vendor Legal Name: BarronCrafts / Cognicopia

3. Schedule automated, courteous payment tracking follow-ups at Day 15, Day 25, and Day 30 until invoice clearance.
4. Supply standard W-9 / Vendor Packet documentation whenever requested by facility Accounts Payable.
