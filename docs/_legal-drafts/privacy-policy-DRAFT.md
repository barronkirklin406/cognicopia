## 1. Who we are
Cognicopia is published by [LEGAL NAME OF THE CONTRACTING PARTY], [CITY, STATE]. Contact: cognicopia@outlook.com.

## 2. The short version
Cognicopia has no server, account or cloud copy of what staff type about residents. Resident details stay in the browser on the computer where they were typed. We do not receive, store or process them.

## 3. Information we do not collect
- Resident names, birth years, health details, notes, photos or any other information entered into the tools.
- Staff names or progress in the Academy.
- Analytics, advertising identifiers or behaviour tracking. The tools set no cookies.

## 4. Information that stays on your device
The tools keep drafts, profiles, queues, notes, the facility portal, the Shift board and Academy records in the browser's encrypted storage on your computer (AES-256-GCM, with an optional passphrase). You control it. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes it. We cannot see it or recover it.

## 5. Information we may receive
- **Messages and requests.** If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. Please do not include resident information.
- **Purchases.** Card payments are handled by Stripe. We receive a payment reference, the plan, the amount and the business name or email you give. We do not receive card numbers. For an invoice, we receive the billing contact you give us.
- **License keys.** A key holds a business name, a plan and two dates. It is checked on your computer.

## 6. Service providers
- **GitHub** hosts the pages. As host, GitHub receives ordinary web request information such as IP address and browser type, under its own privacy statement.
- **Stripe** processes card payments under its own terms and privacy policy.
- **Email provider** carries messages you send us. [Counsel: name it if required.]
We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.
