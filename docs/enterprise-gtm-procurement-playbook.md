## 4. Turnover-Resistant Account Administration

A chronic challenge in senior living is high frontline employee turnover (averaging 65–85% annually in long-term care). If software accounts are tied to an individual director's email (`sarah.jenkins@facility.com`), access is severed when the employee departs, disrupting resident care and triggering subscription abandonment.

### 4.1 Station & Role-Based Licensing Framework
Cognicopia replaces named-seat employee licensing with **Facility Station & Role-Based Licensing**:

1. **Station Master License:** The subscription is bound to the community building and department role (e.g., `life-enrichment-station1@oakridgecare.com`), rather than an individual staff member.
2. **Encrypted Passphrase Handoff:** Master encryption rings are unlocked via a facility department passphrase (e.g., shared securely in the director's onboarding binder or physical key safe).
3. **Turnover Continuity Workflow:**
   - When a new Activity Director arrives, they simply open the browser on the department computer.
   - All resident profiles, customized wings, and historical packet presets remain encrypted in the local browser database.
   - Entering the department passphrase instantly restores the entire program without waiting for IT password resets or corporate account transfers.
4. **Offline Export / Handoff Archive (`.cognicopia` file):**
   - Outgoing staff or corporate regional directors can export an encrypted station backup file (`station-handoff-wing1.cognicopia`).
   - The incoming director drags this single file onto a new workstation, enters the station passphrase, and the entire roster is immediately populated locally.
