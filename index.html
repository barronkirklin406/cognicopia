Personal paper, made offline

Golden Carers and Activity Connection are online membership libraries: you sign in, then download and print from their ready-made activities. Cognicopia makes personal paper offline: a packet for one resident, from what you know about them, built on your own computer. The packet tools need no internet, and no resident information leaves the device.
