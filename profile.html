Resident Activity Profile

Get to know the person behind the packet

A few short steps about who this resident is: the years they remember best, where they grew up and the work they were proud of. Cognicopia uses it to build activities that feel familiar and respectful.

🔒 Privacy First: All resident information entered here is saved safely and strictly on your local computer. No data is ever extracted, collected, or sent to external servers. We ask these specific questions to generate activities that honor the resident's unique identity.

The profile is kept only in this web browser on this computer. On a shared computer, press Clear this profile when you are finished.

This browser is not allowing Cognicopia to save on this computer (a private window can do this). You can still fill in the profile, but it will be lost when the page closes.

    Life story, career and skills

    Why we ask: Familiar places and lifelong roles are the heart of Montessori-based dementia care. A retired mechanic may enjoy sorting nuts and bolts; a homemaker may enjoy folding and recipe cards. Skills learned over a lifetime often last the longest.

    Choose the work they identify with most, even if they had several jobs.

    Work-related skills

    Check everything they were good at, at work or at home.

    Military service
