Accessibility Statement

What is in place, what is measured, and what is not yet tested

Our aim, and what we have not yet done

Cognicopia is used by Activity Directors, nurses, aides and families, and by people who need large print, strong contrast or a keyboard. We build toward WCAG 2.2 level AA throughout, and hold the text of our design system and the Academy to the stricter 7:1 contrast of level AAA.

We have measured parts of that, as listed below. We have not had the tools reviewed by an independent accessibility auditor, and we have not yet run a full test with people who use screen readers. Until we have, we do not claim conformance with WCAG, and this page is a statement of what is done and what is not. It describes the software as of October 13, 2026.

What is in place

  • Large, easy targets. Every control a finger must hit is at least 52 by 52 pixels, measured in a real browser on every build. A link inside a sentence is the one exception.
  • Contrast. The build measures text against its background on every page, by day and in Night Shift, and fails below WCAG AA (4.5:1). The design system's own text pairs are held to AAA (7:1) and recomputed on every build.
  • Keyboard and focus. Controls can be reached and used from the keyboard, and the focus ring has two bands so it shows on light and dark. The Academy's lesson player answers Space, the arrow keys, Home and End, C for captions, M for mute and F for full screen.
  • Motion. Animation is gentle and stops when the system's reduced-motion setting is on.
  • Type. The interface uses Atkinson Hyperlegible, a typeface designed for low vision, kept on the site so no outside font is loaded.
  • Night Shift. A low-light mode for night staff, checked for contrast like the day mode.
  • Captions and transcripts. Every Academy lesson has live captions and a full transcript that follows along, and lessons count as watched when read.
  • Printed pages for people with low vision. Pages print pure black on white, with lines at least 3 points thick, a wide margin on the binding side, and three support levels. The Advanced level uses the largest type: about 26 points on most pages and larger on some.
  • No colour alone. Completion in the Academy is shown by a check mark and words as well as colour.

Known gaps

  • There is no separate text-size setting yet. For larger print, choose the Advanced stage, and use High Contrast Mode where it helps.
  • Answer keys cannot be switched off on puzzle pages; they print in a marked place that can be folded under.
  • The Academy reads lessons aloud with the computer's own voices, so how natural they sound depends on the voices installed on that computer. Captions and the transcript are always there.
  • Some screens of the Packet Builder are dense. They pass the size and contrast measures above, but we have not tested them with a screen reader, and some may be harder to use that way than they should be.

Tell us what does not work

If something here is hard to use, write to cognicopia@outlook.com and say what you were trying to do, the page, and what got in the way. We read every message. If you need a page in another format, such as a larger print version, tell us and we will work out how to make it.

For the measures behind this page, see the vendor questions and the repository's docs/design-system.md.
