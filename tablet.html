Tablet Activities

Hand over the tablet, or sit side by side. Every activity answers to a finger: slide along a word, trace a path, tap letters on a keyboard that appears on the screen. Large print, calm sounds, nothing to read first, and no internet needed. Nothing anyone does here is stored or sent anywhere.
